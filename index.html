Frequently
asked questions

What is Userled?
Userled is the AI marketing platform for enterprise B2B. AI agents plan, launch and optimise campaigns across paid media, email, web, events and sales, and measure every account from pipeline generation to closed-won.
How is Userled different from an ABM tool?
ABM is one play. Userled runs 1:1, 1:few and 1:many plays for demand gen, field marketing and sales from one platform, and ties every touch to pipeline and revenue in your CRM.
What do we need to get started?
A connection to Salesforce or HubSpot. You can add website, intent and warehouse data whenever you like. Most teams launch their first journey within a day.
Do the AI agents act without approval?
No. You set the goals, budgets and guardrails. Agents propose plays and optimisations, and nothing launches until someone on your team approves it.
Which channels does Userled run on?
LinkedIn and Google Ads, email from account owners, personalised web pages, event invites, Salesforce tasks for sales and Slack alerts, all coordinated in one journey.
How do we measure results?
Every touch is synced to Salesforce opportunities, so you see pipeline influenced, meetings booked and closed-won revenue per account, play and channel.
How is our data kept secure?
Access is controlled with SSO and role-based permissions, and your data is never used to train shared models. Our security team can share full details on request.
